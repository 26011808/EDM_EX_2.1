EX.NO. 2.1
CREATE A 3D SOLID MODEL FROM ISOMETRIC DRAWING

AIM:
To create a 3D model from the given isometric drawing using Fusion 360 tool.

REQUIREMENTS:

1. Autodesk Fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16GB of RAM (Integrated graphics recommend 1GB or more).
4. (2.5Mbps) or faster download; 500Kbps or faster upload.

PROCEDURE:

Step 1 – Preparation of Sheet and Reference Lines

1. First, fix the drawing sheet properly onto the drawing board using clips or drafting tape.
2. Draw a suitable border around the sheet and prepare the title block at the bottom-right corner.
3. Identify the direction marked as "FRONT" in the given diagram and arrange the views accordingly.
4. Draw the Front View (FV) by looking in the direction of the arrow.
5. Project the Top View (TV) directly below the Front View, maintaining proper alignment.
6. Draw the Right Side View (SV) on the left or right side of the Front View according to the given projection method (First Angle or Third Angle Projection).
7. Usually, First Angle Projection is followed unless otherwise specified in the question.

Step 2 – Drawing the Front View (FV)

1. First, draw the main body with an overall width of 100 mm and a height of 50 mm.
2. Draw the rectangular outline of the main body using the given dimensions.
3. Locate the central cylindrical lens (Ø50) on the front face.
4. Keep the lens centre aligned horizontally with the middle of the 100 mm body and draw a circle with a diameter of 50 mm.
5. Draw the top attachments visible in the Front View:
Draw the central trapezoidal flash/viewfinder block with a 45° sloping edge.
Add the outlines of the smaller buttons and dials on the top surface.
6. Finally, darken all the visible outlines using an HB pencil.

Step 3 – Drawing the Top View (TV)

1. First, draw vertical projection lines downwards from the major features of the Front View to maintain proper alignment.
2. Mark the total depth of the object as 55 mm, which includes 35 mm for the main body and 20 mm for the lens projection.
3. Draw the rectangular top profile of the camera body using the dimensions 100 mm × 35 mm.
4. Show the cylindrical lens projecting forward by drawing its corresponding projection boundaries up to 20 mm.
5. Draw the square button block and circular dial (Ø20) on the top surface according to the given offset dimensions.
6. Maintain the correct spacing of approximately 15 mm wherever specified in the drawing.

Step 4 – Drawing the Side View (SV)

1. Use a 45° miter line from the Top View or horizontal projection lines from the Front View to construct the Side View.
2. Mark the overall depth of the main body as 35 mm and maintain the height of 50 mm, including the top attachments as shown in the given drawing.
3. Draw the side profile of the camera body according to the given dimensions.
4. Show the lens projecting 20 mm forward from the main body.
5. Draw the top attachments in their correct positions, showing how they are placed on the upper surface.
6. Finally, darken the visible outlines and check the alignment of all three views.

Step 5 – Dimensioning and Final Finishing

1. Add all the important dimensions clearly using thin dimension lines, extension lines and arrowheads.
2. Mark the overall length as 100 mm, height as 50 mm and body depth as 35 mm.
3. Mention the lens diameter as Ø50 mm, lens projection as 20 mm and dial diameter as Ø20 mm.
4. Represent any hidden internal features using dashed lines wherever necessary.
5. Remove all unnecessary construction lines and check the dimensions with the reference drawing.
6. Finally, darken all the visible object outlines and make sure the drawing is neat and accurate.
